Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

This policy explains how Dad-a-Base: Dad Jokes ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. In short: we don't collect any personal information, and the app does not send any data off your device.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers, usage data, or anything else about you.

Information stored on your device

The app saves two kinds of information locally on your device so it can work the way you expect:

  • Favorite jokes: the jokes you mark with a heart.
  • Your settings: whether the punchline countdown and read-aloud options are on, and the countdown length.

This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. It is removed when you delete the app.

Internet and third parties

The app does not connect to the internet. All jokes and voice recordings are included in the app itself. The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking code, and we do not share any information with third parties because we do not collect any.

Sharing a joke

If you choose to share a joke, the app uses the standard iOS share sheet. What happens next depends on the app you pick (for example Messages or Mail) and is governed by that app's own privacy policy. We don't receive any information when you share.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either. The app contains no ads, no links to the web, and no in-app purchases.

Apple

If you downloaded the app from the App Store, Apple may collect information under its own privacy policy, such as download and purchase records. We do not receive personal information from Apple.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/TMcBuilds/dadjokes-support/issues or visit our Support page.
