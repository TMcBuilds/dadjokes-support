Frequently asked questions

How do I hear the punchline?

Tap Tell me the punchline on any joke. Tap Another one! (or shake your device) for a new joke.

How do I make the app read jokes aloud?

Tap the speaker icon on any joke to hear it. To have every joke read automatically, open Settings (the gear icon at the top left of the main screen) and turn on Read jokes aloud. The voice is built into the app, so it works without an internet connection.

I don't hear anything when I tap the speaker.

Turn up your volume and make sure the device isn't connected to a Bluetooth speaker that's switched off. The app plays audio even when the ring/silent switch is on silent. If it still doesn't work, force-quit the app and open it again, then let us know on GitHub.

What is the punchline countdown?

In Settings, turn on Countdown to punchline and choose 3, 5 or 10 seconds. The punchline appears when the countdown ends. Tap Show it now to skip ahead at any time.

How do I save my favorite jokes?

Tap the heart on a joke. Your saved jokes appear in the Favorites tab. Swipe a joke to the left in that list to remove it.

Will I lose my favorites if I delete the app or get a new phone?

Favorites and settings are stored only on your device, because the app doesn't use accounts or cloud storage. Deleting the app removes them, and they don't transfer automatically to a new device unless you restore it from a device backup.

What is the Joke of the Day?

Tap the calendar icon at the top right of the main screen. It shows one joke per calendar day, and it's the same joke for everyone.

Can I search for a joke?

Yes. Open the Browse tab and use the search bar. It searches both the setup and the punchline. You can also browse by category.

How do I share a joke?

Tap the share icon on any joke and pick Messages, Mail, or any other app.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. All jokes and voice recordings are included in the app.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 18 or later.

Can you add my joke or a new category?

We'd love to hear your ideas. Send suggestions through the GitHub link above.
